About me

I'm a Senior Product Designer in São Paulo, at Itaú Unibanco — one of the largest banks in Latin America. Seven years in, across digital and physical products.

I design high-volume journeys: customer acquisition, service channels and loyalty programmes. In practice that means account opening and credit card applications, gamification mechanics for the bank's relationship programme, and research into the accessibility barriers retired INSS beneficiaries meet when they use financial products. Regulated work, with compliance in the room from the start rather than at the end.

I came to screens from objects. Before the bank I was at Klabin developing smart, sustainable packaging — IoT, sensors, microcontrollers — and before that I researched ergonomic products and interfaces at the Ergonomics and Interfaces Lab at Unesp, where I took my B.A. in Product Design and co-authored a study on self-image in people who use lower-limb prostheses. The interest in accessibility started there, not at the bank.

I'm reading for a B.A. in Philosophy at USP alongside the work. What holds both together is design engineering — the seam where interface decisions stop being drawings and start being behaviour.
